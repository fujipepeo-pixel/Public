A guide for international shoppers

Unique Finds From Japan

Most of this site is written in Japanese for a Japanese audience, tracking real-time stock at Rakuten and Yahoo! Shopping. This page is different: it's a short, honest guide in English to a few shops that ship the real thing — made or sourced in Japan — internationally.

Before you shop: import rules (customs duties, restricted items) vary by country, so it's worth checking your own country's rules before ordering, especially for anything unusual.
